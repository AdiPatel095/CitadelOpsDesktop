# 2.4.0 — release integration

This document tracks the changes queued for the 2.4.0 release. The source candidate is version 2.4.0.
These notes are not a deployment receipt. Stable desktop downloads, workers,
and the portal retain their separately verified production artifacts.

## Public release summary

### Added

- Auto Fortress attacks freshly available outer-kingdom fortresses within saved limits, distributes purchased Direwolves across enabled castles, and can use guarded Time Skips for its own confirmed transfers.
- Auto Booster optionally buys the daily fortress-speed offer while preserving your saved ruby reserve and confirming the active effect.
- Auto Towers supports Robber Baron Advisor chains, optional activation with a dedicated token, and saved daily Time Skip limits.
- Auto Bird adds per-castle pause, timed pause and resend controls, runtime preset selection and scheduled preset changes. Enabled Fortress castles keep their reserved Direwolves at home.
- Feature Stats shows final known scores from completed collected events, with dates, ranks and filtering. Unknown scores stay unknown; account scores are not presented as points earned by automation.

### Fixed

- Auto Bird resumes reliably after support troops return and handles larger troop mixes; premium commander movements no longer stall Auto Towers.
- Auto Khan keeps retaliation movements and results visible, follows current camp and rage state, and avoids interference from unrelated support movements.
- Auto Buyer and Khan purchases use the current offer and intended castle. Buyer keeps valid feast and specialist goals working independently, respects reserves, and checks uncertain purchases before trying again.
- Food deliveries use the correct marketplace castle, and returning trade carts remain reserved until they arrive home.
- Nomad cooldown skips use fresh cooldown information, and sequential attacks preserve their intended arrival order.
- Auto TCI respects attached construction items even when their timers reach zero, and waits when slot information is stale or incomplete.
- Coin spending waits until the latest confirmed balance covers the full command cost, saved reserve and other pending spending.
- Hosted settings preserve in-progress drafts and preset save baselines through background updates; invalid drafts cannot start an action.

### Changed

- Equipment Reconfigure compares up to 10 useful distinct alternatives with complete capped totals and compatible equipment, heroes and gems. Apply uses the selected extraction costs and maximum ruby charge you accepted.
- Storm and Berimond builders follow ordered construction phases. A blocked phase does not spend on later construction, and Storm warns when required stored decorations are missing.
- Automation safety pauses expire 30 minutes after the original rejection, including across restarts. Supported duplicate alliance-help responses no longer create unnecessary pauses.

## Engineering history (includes work already deployed separately)

The following integration details preserve earlier implementation evidence; they
are not a list of newly introduced 2.4.0 features.

## Added

- Settings-only stable/beta switching: each VM keeps its own profile and local
  history; only the latest canonical settings are applied at the destination.
  Exact ownership, source stop and target readiness remain mandatory. Existing
  in-flight archive operations can finish without altering their journals.

- Auto Bird's castle hover supports click-to-pause/resume, a right-click or
  timer-button duration picker (1 minute to 7 days), and a per-castle resend
  button. Pauses persist independently of cycle tracking and never edit
  settings or presets. Timed pauses resume automatically. Resend clears only
  the chosen cycle, then runs fresh AIN target discovery and JAA troop
  preparation; existing game movements continue, and global enablement,
  schedules, protection, and lane locks still apply.

- Feature Stats shows retained final known event scores in the related event
  feature tabs, with completed runs newest-first, event filtering, local end
  dates, rank, and pagination. The Events tab also exposes all collected event
  types. Scores are scoped to the bound player and world and remain explicitly
  account totals, not automation-attributed points. Uncollected or unknown
  scores are not invented; history uses the existing World Intelligence feed.

- Equipment Reconfigure produces up to 10 ranked, distinct alternatives for
  one selected commander or castellan. Generated choices remain available for
  immediate comparison, and applying a preview uses only the selected result.

- Auto Bird derives a locked All Direwolves reserve for every enabled Auto
  Fortress outer-kingdom main castle. The derived row follows current global,
  timed, kingdom, and castle controls without being written into Auto Bird
  ignore settings or presets; a saved manual Direwolf quantity remains intact
  underneath it.

## Fixed

- Fixed Auto Buyer and Auto Booster actions stopping before execution because
  their resource claims were not recognized.

- Expired support with a regular commander, including commander zero, no longer
  survives scoped GAM omission indefinitely. Its hold ends after arrival,
  station wait, return travel, and the commander settle grace. The movement
  clock schedules that boundary, and expired cached support cannot block a
  fresh Auto Bird cycle after the bird list is cleared. Unknown timing remains
  conservative; active support is retained.
- Carry the deployed CDS batching fix into 2.4.0: at most 10 troop types per
  command, sequential acknowledged batches, and no replay of completed batches.
  Stop on a rejected or uncertain batch and recheck fresh units before sending.
  The blanket CDS block is removed.
- Castle control revisions invalidate earlier target captures and prepared
  sends. Every Auto Bird batch checks the current pause and control revision,
  so a queued old cycle cannot overwrite a resend or continue after a pause.

- Previous event history normalizes websocket world addresses to the canonical
  cloud world identity while preserving player isolation and distinct ports.
  Valid collected final scores no longer fail the identity check on beta.

- Feature Stats no longer presents an expired cached event as currently running.
  Live cards honor score expiry, session freshness, and newer event inventory;
  open views update when an event ends without a manual reload. Desktop and
  hosted frontend behavior is aligned. Queued for 2.4.0, not deployed.

- Duplicate alliance-help rejections (`AHR 273`, official
  `NO_MULTIPLE_ALLIANCEHELP`) no longer create an automation lane safety lock.
  The rejection remains visible as a failed operation; no new automatic retry
  is introduced. Whitelisted pairs also release any saved lane lock during
  startup refresh.
- Every non-whitelisted rejection, including CRA 256, MSD and unknown errors,
  locks only its originating lane for 30 minutes from the original rejection.
  Restarting does not reset the timer. Saved legacy locks are refreshed
  durably; expired locks become eligible again without manual approval.

- Premium-commander movements using the game-provided `UM.L.DLID` leader
  identity are retained alongside ordinary `UM.L.ID` movements. Support and
  other movement types no longer invalidate an otherwise complete GAM
  snapshot and stall Auto Towers. Preserve negative leader sentinels without
  assigning an owned commander; malformed leader data remains guarded.

- Auto Food logistics refresh now re-enters the marketplace castle after a
  world-map lookup before requesting market capacity (`CMI`). It validates
  live castle context instead of relying only on the cached focused flag,
  preventing the observed `CMI 262` (`NO_MARKETPLACE`) context rejection.
  Marketplace eligibility and lane safety remain unchanged; 262 is not
  whitelisted. This fix is queued for 2.4.0, not deployed as a tenant hotfix.

- Returning marketplace carts remain reserved at their home castle until
  they arrive back. Auto Sceat resource logistics and Auto Food no longer
  treat carts as available when the game reverses movement endpoints for
  the return trip, preventing the observed `CRM 109` (`MISSING_CARRIAGE`)
  rejection. CRM 109 remains guarded. Queued for 2.4.0; no tenant hotfix.

- Auto Buyer isolates invalid shop and specialist goals so they cannot block
  valid feast upkeep, and evaluates due feasts before specialist refreshes.
  The settings screen permits feast edits while preserving unchanged obsolete
  goals; new or changed spending goals still require validation. Unresolved
  feast purchases use paced read-only checks, recovering early only after two
  distinct current-connection replies explicitly confirm inactivity, at least
  30 seconds apart and after a 30-second settling period. Omitted, malformed,
  duplicate, or conflicting active responses cannot establish that recovery.
  Food reserves, live preflight checks, purchase pacing, ruby-feast restrictions,
  and lane safety remain enforced. Queued for 2.4.0; no tenant hotfix.

- Auto Buyer feast upkeep maintains the captured type-8 feast strictly above
  its saved duration floor, automatically choosing the eligible owned
  positive-net castle with the most stored food. Each
  sequential purchase rechecks castle identity, food, reserves, authoritative
  timer state, and refreshed resources; stale or uncertain state cannot spend.

- All six resource overseers, Marauder, Tax Collector, and Drill Instructor
  renew independently in one-week increments at or below their saved duration
  floors. Fresh timer and ruby observations, validated per-purchase ceilings,
  the global reserve, and durable unresolved-purchase recovery govern dispatch.

- Auto Booster accepts the captured empty success acknowledgement and confirms
  effect 2 from occurrence-bound boosted status in either message order. It
  retains the authoritative event expiry and suppresses repeat spending for the
  same occurrence across reconnects and restarts.

- Equipment previews remain visible across unrelated live updates, become
  explicitly stale after relevant inventory changes, and reject obsolete or
  repeated apply requests. Alternatives include meaningful item and grouped
  stat comparisons for both commander and castellan flows.

- Equipment Reconfigure keeps ordinary and relic equipment, heroes, and gems
  in compatible loadouts. The optimizer searches both valid families, retains
  a socketed appearance item's family restriction, and rejects incompatible or
  stale selections before changing equipment.

- Equipment Reconfigure scores complete official effect groups across equipped
  items, heroes, gems, set bonuses, caps, combat mode, and target type. It keeps
  materially distinct useful alternatives instead of filling the list with
  dominated or duplicate assignments, and shows the current, proposed, and
  difference totals for each option.

- Auto Fortress balances available purchased Direwolves across enabled outer
  kingdom main castles. It counts confirmed inbound troops, refreshes arrivals
  before reuse or attack, and can apply guarded official time skips only to its
  own confirmed transfers when that option is enabled. Transfer uncertainty is
  persisted before dispatch and reconciled from current-session evidence.

- Auto Booster ingests global-effect availability and quotes from trigger event
  records. Partial snapshots no longer erase a valid current trigger or create
  authority for an offer the trigger did not contain.

- Auto Fortress binds every attack to a committed exact-target availability
  response, the active connection, session, focus, operation, and response
  token. It opens the official kingdom-fortress attack dialog before launch;
  empty, stale, mismatched, or malformed replies fail closed.

- Kingdom troop reconciliation carries the complete source castle, target
  kingdom, troop, transfer, and expected-state arguments through action-plan
  rebuilding, so final guards inspect the intended shipment instead of an
  incomplete replacement step.

- Auto Bird removes every current and future Direwolf from fresh support
  manifests for enabled Auto Fortress outer-kingdom main castles. Every frozen
  batch rechecks the derived reserve at final transport dispatch, so enabling
  Fortress after preparation cannot send protected troops. Other units and
  disabled kingdoms remain eligible, and disabling Fortress restores the saved
  manual reserve on the next fresh cycle.

## Security

- Premium automation revalidates current-session resource authority, price,
  reserve, settings, timer, and occurrence state after outbound queue and
  transport waits. Durable feast, specialist, and global-booster records keep
  accepted or uncertain sends from being replayed blindly.

- Equipment Reconfigure derives ordinary-gem extraction charges from the same
  transition it will execute and the current official gem catalog. Every
  alternative shows its extraction count and maximum ruby charge; Apply sends
  explicit consent only for that selected ceiling. The planner and final
  transport boundary recheck the selected equipment, exact gems, and official
  prices before every ordinary-gem extraction, including one priced at zero.
  The paid path also rechecks the current-session ruby balance and
  affordability before each extraction. An uncertain paid extraction remains
  durably blocked from replay. Coin socketing and relic-fragment extraction
  costs are disclosed without inventing an unverified fragment amount.

- Fortress attack and Auto Bird support transports revalidate authoritative
  target, session, focus, configuration, and protected-troop state after queue
  waits. Missing correlation or changed protection stops stale work for a fresh
  rebuild without weakening existing lane locks or rejection handling.
